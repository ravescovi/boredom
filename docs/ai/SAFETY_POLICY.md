# AI Safety Policy

Bordon.ai v0 must reject or avoid:

- Drinking games and intoxication mechanics.
- Gambling, betting, wagering, lotteries, and financial stakes.
- Physical-risk mechanics.
- Dangerous dares, stunts, pain, restraint, weapons, or unsafe movement.
- IP infringement or imitation of existing games and franchises.
- Commercialization of generated games.

Every generated game must be schema-valid, original, non-commercial, and playable without dangerous materials or unsafe physical actions.
